Adult Cardiothoracic Anesthesiology

High Yield / Impactful Articles


Study Design

Understanding RCTs
Take Home Message: Randomization, allocation concealment, and blinded outcome assessment help readers judge whether a trial’s treatment effect is credible and applicable.

How to Read a Meta-Analysis
Take Home Message: Interpret pooled effects alongside study quality, heterogeneity, and confidence intervals before applying a meta-analysis to patient care.


Anesthestic Agents and Approaches

MYRIAD
Take Home Message: Volatile anesthetics did not reduce one-year mortality compared with total intravenous anesthesia in patients undergoing elective CABG.

SOLVE TAVI
Take Home Message: Conscious sedation and general anesthesia produced equivalent rates of the composite 30-day outcome in selected patients undergoing transfemoral TAVR.

DEXACET
Take Home Message: Scheduled intravenous acetaminophen reduced in-hospital delirium compared with placebo in older adults undergoing cardiac surgery in this single-center trial.

EEG Burst Suppression Mediates Delirium
Take Home Message: EEG burst suppression during cardiopulmonary bypass was associated with postoperative delirium in older patients, but this observational mediation analysis does not establish causation.

ENGAGES-Canada
Take Home Message: EEG-guided anesthetic administration reduced anesthetic exposure and EEG suppression but did not reduce delirium after cardiac surgery in older adults.


Perioperative Medical Therapy

POISE
Take Home Message: Starting extended-release metoprolol shortly before noncardiac surgery reduced myocardial infarction but increased stroke and death.

POISE II
Take Home Message: Perioperative aspirin did not reduce death or nonfatal myocardial infarction after noncardiac surgery and increased major bleeding.

POISE III
Take Home Message: Tranexamic acid reduced major bleeding in noncardiac surgery, but noninferiority for the composite cardiovascular safety outcome was not established.

Variation in Inotrope use in Cardiac Surgery
Take Home Message: Inotrope use during cardiac surgery varied substantially by clinician and institution even after adjustment for patient characteristics.


Fluids and Blood Products

ALBICS
Take Home Message: Using 4% albumin rather than Ringer acetate for bypass priming and perioperative volume replacement did not reduce major adverse events after cardiac surgery.

PCC vs FFP for post-cardiotomy bleeding
Take Home Message: In this small randomized trial, PCC did not significantly reduce postoperative chest-tube drainage compared with plasma, although some transfusion outcomes favored PCC.

FARES-II, PCC vs FFP
Take Home Message: PCC achieved better hemostatic efficacy and reduced transfusion requirements compared with frozen plasma for coagulopathic bleeding after cardiopulmonary bypass.

ANH: Acute Normovolemic Hemodilution in Cardiac Surgery; NEJM 2025
Take Home Message: Acute normovolemic hemodilution did not significantly reduce the proportion of cardiac surgery patients receiving allogeneic red-cell transfusions.


Aortic Valve

Evolut Low Risk
Take Home Message: Self-expanding TAVR was noninferior to surgery for death or disabling stroke at two years in patients with severe aortic stenosis at low surgical risk.

Partner III
Take Home Message: Balloon-expandable TAVR reduced the one-year composite of death, stroke, or rehospitalization compared with surgery in selected patients at low surgical risk.

PROTECTED TAVR
Take Home Message: Cerebral embolic protection did not significantly reduce the primary outcome of periprocedural stroke during TAVR.


Mitral Valve

CTSN Ischemic MR
Take Home Message: Repair and chordal-sparing replacement produced similar one-year survival and ventricular remodeling in severe ischemic mitral regurgitation, but recurrent regurgitation was more frequent after repair.

RIME
Take Home Message: Adding mitral annuloplasty to CABG improved exercise capacity and ventricular remodeling in moderate ischemic mitral regurgitation in this small trial stopped early.

Surgical LAA occlusion to prevent stroke
Take Home Message: Concomitant left atrial appendage occlusion reduced ischemic stroke or systemic embolism in patients with atrial fibrillation undergoing cardiac surgery, most of whom continued anticoagulation.


Tricuspid Valve

CTSN Tricuspid Repair in Mitral Regurg
Take Home Message: Adding tricuspid annuloplasty to degenerative mitral surgery reduced progression of tricuspid regurgitation but increased permanent pacemaker implantation.

TRILUMINATE Pivotal
Take Home Message: Tricuspid transcatheter edge-to-edge repair improved quality of life but did not clearly reduce death or heart-failure hospitalization at one year.

Tricuspid Regurgitation - NEJM 2023 Review
Take Home Message: Assessment of tricuspid regurgitation should integrate severity, right ventricular function, and underlying cause to guide medical, surgical, or transcatheter treatment.


Heart Failure, Mechanical Support, and Transplants

MOMENTUM III
Take Home Message: In extended observational follow-up of MOMENTUM 3, HeartMate 3 was associated with better five-year survival free of disabling stroke or pump replacement than HeartMate II.

Mild AI at time of LVAD
Take Home Message: Uncorrected mild aortic insufficiency at LVAD implantation was associated with progressive regurgitation and more heart-failure readmissions, without a demonstrated survival difference.

Transplantation Outcomes with Donor Hearts after Circulatory Death
Take Home Message: Transplantation using reanimated donor hearts after circulatory death was noninferior to transplantation using hearts after brain death for six-month risk-adjusted survival.

INCEPTION: Early Extracorporeal CPR for Refractory Out-of-Hospital Cardiac Arrest
Take Home Message: Early extracorporeal CPR did not significantly improve 30-day survival with favorable neurologic function compared with conventional CPR in refractory out-of-hospital arrest.

ECLS-SHOCK: Extracorporeal Life Support in Infarct-Related Cardiogenic Shock
Take Home Message: Routine early extracorporeal life support did not reduce 30-day mortality in infarct-related cardiogenic shock and increased bleeding and vascular complications.


CABG

Radial Artery Patency
Take Home Message: Radial-artery grafts had better one-year patency than saphenous-vein grafts, particularly when the target coronary artery had severe stenosis.

Arterial Revascularization
Take Home Message: Bilateral internal thoracic artery grafting did not significantly improve ten-year survival compared with single internal thoracic artery grafting in the intention-to-treat analysis.

ROOBY
Take Home Message: At five years, off-pump CABG had higher mortality and more major adverse cardiovascular events than on-pump CABG in the ROOBY population.

GOPCABE
Take Home Message: Among patients aged 75 years or older, off-pump and on-pump CABG produced similar five-year survival and rates of major adverse cardiovascular events.

DOORS
Take Home Message: Off-pump CABG produced lower six-month graft patency than on-pump CABG in older patients despite comparable heparinization protocols.

VISION
Take Home Message: Postoperative high-sensitivity troponin I levels associated with increased 30-day mortality after cardiac surgery were substantially higher than commonly used myocardial-injury thresholds.


Major Aorta Surgery

Spinal Cord Protection - Review
Take Home Message: Spinal cord protection during thoracoabdominal aortic surgery requires a multimodal strategy that preserves cord perfusion and addresses ischemic risk throughout the perioperative period.

Pathophysiology of Aortic Cross-Clamping and Unclamping
Take Home Message: Aortic cross-clamping increases ventricular afterload and redistributes blood flow, while unclamping can cause profound hypotension from reduced vascular resistance and reperfusion effects.


Lung Injury

STRESS
Take Home Message: In infants undergoing cardiac surgery, prophylactic methylprednisolone did not significantly improve the adjusted primary outcome and increased insulin-treated hyperglycemia.

SIRS
Take Home Message: Perioperative methylprednisolone did not reduce 30-day mortality or major morbidity in higher-risk adults undergoing cardiac surgery with cardiopulmonary bypass.

DECS
Take Home Message: High-dose dexamethasone did not significantly reduce the primary composite of major adverse events after cardiac surgery.

MECANO
Take Home Message: Continuing low-tidal-volume ventilation during cardiopulmonary bypass did not significantly reduce the primary composite of death and postoperative respiratory complications.

Ventilation during CPB Meta-analysis
Take Home Message: Ventilation during cardiopulmonary bypass improved immediate gas exchange in pooled small trials but did not clearly reduce postoperative pulmonary complications or hospital stay.

Intensive Recruitment Strategy RCT
Take Home Message: An intensive recruitment strategy reduced the severity of pulmonary complications compared with moderate recruitment in hypoxemic patients after cardiac surgery in this single-center trial.

PROVECS
Take Home Message: An open-lung ventilation strategy did not significantly reduce postoperative pulmonary complications compared with conventional ventilation in elective on-pump cardiac surgery.

PROVHILO
Take Home Message: High PEEP with recruitment maneuvers did not reduce pulmonary complications after open abdominal surgery and caused more hypotension than low PEEP.

IMPROVE
Take Home Message: A protective ventilation bundle using low tidal volumes, PEEP, and recruitment maneuvers reduced major complications after abdominal surgery compared with high tidal volumes without PEEP.

High vs Low PEEP in Obese Non-Cardiac Surgery
Take Home Message: High PEEP with recruitment maneuvers did not significantly reduce postoperative pulmonary complications in obese surgical patients and increased intraoperative hemodynamic adverse events.

Driving Pressure in Thoracic Surgery
Take Home Message: Individualizing PEEP to minimize driving pressure reduced postoperative pulmonary complications during one-lung ventilation in this randomized thoracic surgery trial.

iPROVE-OLV: Individualised Open-Lung ventilation during OLV Multicenter RCT
Take Home Message: An individualized open-lung strategy reduced severe postoperative pulmonary complications compared with conventional protective ventilation in patients undergoing lung resection with one-lung ventilation.

Driving Pressure in Abdominal Surgery
Take Home Message: PEEP individualized to the lowest driving pressure reduced clinically significant pulmonary complications after open upper abdominal surgery in this small single-center trial.

Mechanical Power and Postoperative Respiratory Failure: Multicenter Retrospective Cohort Study
Take Home Message: Higher intraoperative mechanical power was associated with postoperative respiratory failure requiring reintubation, without proving that lowering mechanical power prevents it.

PRIME-AIR
Take Home Message: A perioperative lung-expansion bundle did not reduce pulmonary complication severity compared with usual care in at-risk adults undergoing major open abdominal surgery.


Inflammation

The Systemic Inflammatory Response to Cardiac Surgery: Implications for the Anesthesiologist
Take Home Message: Cardiopulmonary bypass triggers interacting inflammatory pathways that can contribute to organ dysfunction, supporting a broad understanding of perioperative injury rather than a single-target explanation.

Phenotypes of CPB-induced Inflammatory Response
Take Home Message: Three clinical phenotypes of the bypass-associated inflammatory response differed in biomarker levels and outcomes, providing a basis for future individualized-treatment research.


Echocardiography

Association of E/e’ with CV Events Retrospective Cohort
Take Home Message: Higher echocardiographic E/e’ was associated with cardiovascular events across a diverse clinical population, supporting its role as a risk marker rather than a standalone diagnosis.

Mild AI at time of LVAD
Take Home Message: Uncorrected mild aortic insufficiency at LVAD implantation was associated with progressive regurgitation and more heart-failure readmissions, without a demonstrated survival difference.
